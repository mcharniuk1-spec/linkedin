# Weekly planning questions

Agent prepares candidate posts from the approved monthly strategy and evidence. Ask the founder a short grouped brief:

- What changed in the product, market or team’s understanding this week?
- What did a real buyer/user ask that is worth explaining? Preserve the actual wording only with appropriate permission.
- Which decision, demonstration or useful artifact can we show?
- Which opinion or practical recommendation do you want to stand behind?
- Is any detail inaccurate, confidential, outdated or a promise we cannot make?

Then select the next useful pieces. For each record: audience question; pattern; source IDs; hook/payoff; useful example/tool; visual job; natural ending; author check; next step. Do not require a new anecdote for every slot.

After publication, review the actual relevant replies and available native analytics. Choose one editorial change and one conversation action, with a reason. Do not automatically change the strategy because a single post has fewer reactions.
