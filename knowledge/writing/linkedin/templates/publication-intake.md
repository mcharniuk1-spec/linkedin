# One-publication brief

Agent prefill:

```text
Task / publication ID:
Author and channel:
Reader, situation and decision:
Main useful point:
Pattern and why it fits:
Source IDs and exact supported claims:
Worked example / tool / process:
Hook option A / payoff:
Hook option B / payoff:
Story or reasoning sequence:
Visual job, objects and emphasis:
Ending / question / relevant offer:
Company repost’s additional contribution:
Required fact / voice / design review:
```

Ask only if it affects correctness:

1. **Personal story:** what actually happened, what was your role, what alternatives existed, and what followed?
2. **Author stance:** is this the point you want to make, and which wording would you change?
3. **Product or service claim:** does it work this way today, and what are the meaningful exceptions?
4. **Number or result:** what is the source, period, population, baseline and your actual contribution?
5. **Example rights:** may we name or show it? If not, can a clearly labelled illustration teach the point?

Return a caption, separate repost introduction, useful visual brief, claim map and exact remaining questions. A method post with supported facts does not need an invented interview, customer or personal incident to proceed.
