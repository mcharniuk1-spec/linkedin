# Monthly founder-content strategy brief

Agent: prefill current site/offer, approved buyer context, prior learning and useful source material before asking. Owner answers only the unresolved choices.

1. **Commercial job:** What should the right buyer better understand or do this month? Which offer is relevant?
2. **Buyer situation:** Who has the problem, what triggers it, and which other people influence the decision?
3. **Three decisions:** What must they understand about the problem, the approach and implementation?
4. **Source material:** Which product changes, calls, examples, research, demos or useful failures can we safely use?
5. **Author position:** What will the founder recommend, challenge or explain from their own judgment?
6. **Proof and examples:** Which claims have actual evidence? Which need a source or a labelled illustration?
7. **Content mix:** Choose suitable patterns and formats. Do not turn every idea into a personal story or every post into a carousel.
8. **Conversation path:** Where should the profile, Featured item, post response and offer lead? Which account actions are actually in scope?
9. **Capacity and review:** Who supplies facts, edits, reviews and publishes? What can the founder realistically review?
10. **Learning:** Which observable question will we review at month-end? Keep distribution, comprehension, conversations and revenue separate.

Output: one-page monthly strategy; buyer-question/content-pattern matrix; source/proof queue; planned useful assets; review roles; a limited learning plan. Prices, quotas and account access come from the actual service scope, not this template.
