# What can be prepared automatically, and what needs a person

| Publication type | Repeatable preparation | Required founder/client input | Final human judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
|Diagnostic checklist|Structure, questions, example slots, source linking.|Actual buyer problem and relevant criteria.|Whether it helps this reader; factual boundaries.|
|Workflow walkthrough|Input/step/output canvas, diagram layout, checklist.|Actual process, tools, exceptions and handoffs.|Product behavior, completeness and usability.|
|Product or interface explanation|Annotated example, before/after wording, visual exports.|Real feature, intended user, task and permitted screenshots.|Meaning, accuracy and real utility.|
|Source-led market insight|Find primary source, extract exact measure/context, chart supplied values.|Author’s interpretation and relevance to the offer.|Whether the inference follows; current limitations.|
|Named public case|Source summary, timeline, mechanism questions and attribution.|Any first-hand involvement must be separately confirmed.|Do not turn vendor-reported outcomes into our results.|
|Founder decision or mistake story|Interview questions, outline, editing and visual plan.|The real event, role, alternatives, consequence and present stance.|The person confirms the story and permitted detail.|
|Point of view or analogy|Alternative hooks, argument map, counterexample search.|An opinion the named author actually endorses.|Voice, fairness, specificity and relevance.|
|Message/reply guide|Context/question/artifact/next-step scaffold; variant drafting.|Actual recipient context, relationship, reply and value offered.|Relevance and permission; sending is separate.|
|Offer or early-client invitation|Fit/scope/input/output/terms structure.|Current deliverables, availability, price/terms and fulfilment.|No invented scarcity, guarantee or customer proof.|
|Visual comparison or schema|Original layout, labels, data-to-shape rendering, consistency checks.|Accurate comparison criteria and product details.|Phone-scale readability, truthful emphasis and meaningful objects.|
|Video demonstration|Shot/step/script plan and caption structure.|Real footage or permissioned screen capture and actual behavior.|Watch the scoped export, check speech/screens/rights; frames alone are partial.|
|Weekly learning/reflection|Aggregate permitted metrics and conversation notes; propose next questions.|Actual observations, surprising cases and chosen action.|No causality or sales intent inferred from a public counter.|

Tools may prefill a draft and detect exact errors. They cannot generate a missing personal memory, certify product truth, infer someone’s buying interest, grant account permissions or accept the final publication for its author. Native preview/publication, client interviews, account access and real conversations stay explicit execution steps.
