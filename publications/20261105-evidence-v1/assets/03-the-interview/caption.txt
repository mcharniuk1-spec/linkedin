The sentence a founder needs for a post may not arrive as a polished line.

It may appear when you ask: what did you decide, what else did you consider, and what changed your mind?

I would start an Outspoken interview there. Not to manufacture a dramatic origin story, but to understand a decision the founder can explain accurately. Then we connect that decision to a question a buyer faces.

A useful draft needs the trade-off, the evidence that can be shared, and a clear point for the reader. The founder reviews the final wording and decides what remains private.

If you could explain one decision behind your company to a future buyer, which would it be?
