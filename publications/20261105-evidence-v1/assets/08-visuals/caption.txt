A beautiful visual can still leave me asking: what am I supposed to understand?

Before designing a LinkedIn image, I would choose one question the reader should be able to answer after seeing it.

If the post explains a workflow, show the sequence. If it compares two approaches, make the difference visible. If it makes a claim, put the source or limitation close enough to read.

That is the role we want visuals to play at Outspoken: give a founder’s idea a form a buyer can follow. The design supports the argument; it does not verify the claim for us.

What should a reader understand in five seconds from your next visual?
