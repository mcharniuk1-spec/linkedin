Here is a writing exercise I would use with an AI founder: explain a feature without starting with the model.

Start with the moment a person needs to act.

What triggers the work? What information does the product use? What does it return? Who checks the result—and what can that person do next?

For example, a workflow diagram could show a request coming in, an AI system suggesting an answer, and a person reviewing it before it reaches a customer. That is an illustration, not a claim about a particular product.

Once the sequence is clear, we can choose what belongs in a post, a visual or a short script. The founder checks the technical detail and what can be shared.
