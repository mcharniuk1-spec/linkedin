If every company in your category can make the same claim, the buyer still has the work of figuring out the difference.

Take a generic line like “AI-powered workflows.” It describes a broad category. It does not tell me which buyer it serves, which job it supports, or why your approach is relevant. This is an illustrative phrase, not a customer quotation.

A more useful starting point is a sequence: name the buyer, describe the job, then explain a difference you can substantiate.

That is how I want Outspoken to approach positioning before we turn it into LinkedIn copy and visuals. A sharper sentence should make the next conversation easier to understand. Its commercial effect still needs to be measured.
