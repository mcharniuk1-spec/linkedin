# Outspoken — draft copy and evidence map

**Status:** proposed LinkedIn copy and artwork text for founder review. Audience: Europe-based founders building B2B AI SaaS companies, typically 10–50 people and Seed–Series B. The founder's own buyers must be researched separately. No publication, customer validation or performance outcome is claimed.

The ten IDs continue the previous personal-editorial set. Each has one caption and two visual directions. IDs 01–05 have five slides per direction; IDs 06–10 have one image per direction. The graphic direction uses diagrams and objects. The people direction uses illustrative people and silhouettes; retain a visible **illustrative scene** disclosure on each image containing a generated person. The writing is designed to be read alongside the image rather than repeat a fake LinkedIn interface.

## Protected owner-approved wording

- Hero: **Make the right buyers care about you and what you’re building.**
- Value proposition: **We help Europe-based B2B AI SaaS founders turn their story and the business they’re building into LinkedIn content that attracts the right buyers, earns their trust and gives them a reason to buy.**
- CTA: **Book an intro call**

The copy proposes 10 different arguments: founder reasoning; qualified reading of data; interviewing; positioning; proof; product explanation; founder/company voices; visual clarity; measurement; and delivery. Humanize-writing full-mode review focused on paragraph rhythm, specificity and audience fit without changing measured facts or inventing a founder anecdote. The proposed voice remains provisional because no owner-confirmed anecdote was supplied. The owner should review every first-person statement, any claim of service readiness, imagery and final CTA before use.
