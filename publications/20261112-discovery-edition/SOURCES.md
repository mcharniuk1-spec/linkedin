## Formatting

Plain-text feed captions use short paragraphs, bullets and numbered steps. Bold emphasis appears in native graphics and this review gallery. LinkedIn documents native bold for [articles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a522427), not a short-feed bold button. Third-party Unicode conversion is optional and untested; keep a readable plain-text fallback.
