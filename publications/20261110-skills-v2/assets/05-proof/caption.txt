A screenshot is not a time-saving claim.

Imagine a request-review tool. A hypothetical demonstration could show three sample requests, a stated review rule and the flags the tool would suggest. A person would check those suggestions before any next action.

That would make one part of the proposed workflow inspectable. It would not establish a typical error rate, repeated speed or a reliable customer saving.

For a performance claim, define the task, the comparison, the sample, the measurement period and the human work included. Then report what was actually observed, with its operating conditions.

The example here is illustrative. No product execution, benchmark or customer result is being reported.

Write the claim at the level the evidence can support. A demonstration can show a step. A saving needs a comparable test.
