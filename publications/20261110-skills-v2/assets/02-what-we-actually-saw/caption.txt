107.5 reactions is a number. It is not a posting target.

Our saved reference sample contains 279 selected posts from 61 accounts. Reaction counts were available for 276 posts; their median was 107.5. Comment counts were available for 256; their median was 36.5.

These counters were recorded on 22 October 2026. The sample spans different authors, audiences, topics and publication ages. It is not a picture of everything happening on LinkedIn now.

The numbers describe observed interactions. They do not tell us how many people saw the posts, whether the right buyers cared, or whether anyone bought.

Use the sample to find a question worth testing. Compare similar conditions, keep the denominator, and leave the unsupported conclusion open.

A number is more useful when its limits travel with it.
