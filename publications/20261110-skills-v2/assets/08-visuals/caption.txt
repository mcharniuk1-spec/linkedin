Give the image a question before you give it a style.

Does order matter? Show a sequence. Do differences matter? Show a comparison. Does the claim depend on an observed result? Show the result with the conditions needed to interpret it.

An image can also supply atmosphere. That is a legitimate job; it should not be mistaken for a product demonstration or customer proof.

The fictional people in these draft designs are illustrations. They do not represent customers, employees or founders.

Name the one relationship your next image needs to show. Choose the representation that makes it easier to inspect, and keep the essential explanation in accessible text too.
