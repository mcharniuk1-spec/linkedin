A useful founder interview starts with a choice, not a topic list.

Ask what the person was trying to achieve, what constrained the decision and which other option looked reasonable at the time.

The rejected option should be a real alternative, not the villain hired for the story.

Then separate three things: what the founder chose, what happened afterward, and how they explain it now. Those are related, but they are not interchangeable.

This is a proposed interview method, not a story about a client engagement already delivered. A real post still needs the author's confirmed details and permission to share them.

For the next interview, choose one decision and record the goal, alternatives and information available at the time.
