Two accounts can answer one buyer question without publishing the same answer twice.

A founder can explain why a decision was made: the constraint, the alternative and the trade-off. A company account can add the product detail, source material or demonstration that helps a reader assess it.

That division is an editorial proposal. It is not a claim that founder accounts always earn more reach or leads.

Start with the shared question. Then ask what distinct evidence or explanation each account can contribute. If the second post adds nothing, a different logo is not enough reason to repeat it.

Give the second publication a useful job of its own.
