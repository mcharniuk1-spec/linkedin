## Plan implications

The launch plan's own-profile work needs actual posts and author review. A writing architecture does not complete D2/OUTSP-37, whose definition includes publication. D5/OUTSP-40 requires six branded Figma slides; a textual panel sequence does not satisfy it. B6/OUTSP-23 still needs founder agreement on who owns English editing and factual review, even though the procedure is now specified. The broader B7 scripts, package/pricing decisions and A1 agency calls remain outside this methodology execution.

The draft-ready handoff is therefore: select the source-confirmed structure, fill only available evidence, write a caption in the actual author's voice, check the claim ledger, reconcile panels and captions, then request approval of that exact revision. The [review template](../../docs/linkedin-writing/templates/review.yaml) records hard gates separately from style judgment. A cold-reading check can test whether a separate reader reconstructs the point, support, limit and next action; no such buyer test is claimed here.
