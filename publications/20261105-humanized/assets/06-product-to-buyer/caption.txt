When a founder explains an AI feature, I want to know what happens just before—and just after—the model is used.

What triggers the work? What information goes in? What does the product suggest? Who checks it, and what happens next?

Those details are often more useful to a buyer than another model name. They reveal how the product fits a real process and where responsibility stays with a person.

We help turn that explanation into a post, a diagram or a short script. The format follows the explanation; the founder checks the technical detail before release.
