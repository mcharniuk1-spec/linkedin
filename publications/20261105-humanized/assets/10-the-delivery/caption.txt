A founder should not have to choose between doing the work and explaining it.

That is the service I am building at Outspoken. We start by understanding the founder, the company and the buyer. We agree on the position and the questions worth answering. Then we turn the material into written and visual drafts.

The founder reviews the detail, the voice and what can be shared. Only then is the content ready to post. The review matters: we cannot know the product or the customer better than the people building it.

After publication, the useful questions and available measures inform the next iteration. There is no promise that every post will take off. The aim is to make a founder’s expertise clearer and more useful to the right buyers over time.
