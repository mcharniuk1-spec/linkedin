“We use AI to transform workflows” leaves me with a question: whose workflow, and what changes?

A positioning exercise should make that answer easier to give. At Outspoken, our approach starts with the customer’s task, the alternatives they already use and the difference the product can support with evidence.

Then we test the language against a simple standard: could a buyer explain it back without using our internal vocabulary?

That becomes the foundation for a founder’s profile and content—not just a line in a brand document.

The aim is not to make a company sound bigger. It is to make the reason it matters easier to understand.
