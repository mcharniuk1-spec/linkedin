I do not think a founder profile and a company page need to repeat each other.

The founder can explain the thinking: the decision, the trade-off and what changed their view. The company can make the supporting material easy to find: a product walkthrough, an approved example or a clear explanation of a limitation.

Both should answer questions the same buyer actually has. Otherwise the founder sounds personal while the company sounds like a different business.

That is how we would plan the two channels at Outspoken. We would also check the responses separately: our selected reference sample does not prove that a founder profile causes better results than a company page.
