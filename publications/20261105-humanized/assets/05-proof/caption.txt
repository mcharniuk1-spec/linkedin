“Our AI changes everything” is a hard claim for a buyer to evaluate.

I would rather show the task it performs, what goes in, what comes out and where a person checks the result. If we quote a number, the period, method and denominator should travel with it.

There is a reason to take that work seriously. In the 2024 Edelman–LinkedIn thought-leadership survey, 73% of decision-makers said an organisation’s thought leadership was a more trustworthy basis for assessing its capabilities than its marketing materials and product sheets. That is a survey response, not evidence that one LinkedIn post will win a deal.

Our job is to help founders turn the evidence they can actually share into clear content, with the important limits intact.
