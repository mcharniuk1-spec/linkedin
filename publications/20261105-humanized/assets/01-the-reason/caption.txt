I want Outspoken to solve a specific problem: a founder has spent years building expertise, but a buyer can only see a short product description.

The useful part is often the thinking behind the product. Why was this problem worth solving? What did the team choose not to build? What should a buyer understand before comparing options?

Our starting point is a conversation with the founder. We turn the decisions, trade-offs and explanations they can substantiate into a positioning and content plan. Then we write and design posts for the founder to review.

The aim is to make the right buyers care about you and what you’re building. What do you wish a buyer understood before the first sales call?
