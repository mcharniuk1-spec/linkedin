A founder does not need to arrive with a polished LinkedIn post.

I would rather start with a decision: What did you change? What else did you consider? Why did you choose this route?

Those questions give an editor something a generic prompt cannot supply: the founder’s actual reasoning. From there, we can identify a buyer question, draft an angle and check which details can be shared.

The founder reviews the copy before anything is ready to post. If a detail is confidential or a result cannot be verified, it stays out.

A good interview should leave the founder with less pressure to perform and the reader with a clearer idea of how the company thinks.
