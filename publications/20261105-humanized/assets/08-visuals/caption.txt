Before designing a LinkedIn image, I want to know what the image makes easier to understand.

A simple workflow can show where an AI system stops and human review begins. A comparison can show the trade-off between two approaches. A founder photograph can put a person behind a point of view.

If a graphic cannot make its relationship clear without a long explanation, it probably needs another edit.

Our process pairs the written argument with the visual brief, then keeps the founder’s review in the loop. We do not treat a polished image as evidence that the underlying claim is true.
