# Editorial and evidence notes

## Audience and voice

The drafts speak to Europe-based founders of B2B AI SaaS companies (10–50 people; Seed to Series B). They present Outspoken's proposed service: interview the founder, clarify positioning and buyer questions, prepare written and visual content, obtain founder review, and use actual responses to guide later iterations. The available user brief establishes the value proposition, not completed client work or client outcomes.

The explicitly requested `humanize-writing` skill was applied in full mode. The edit replaces generic writing advice and repeated three-step explanations with a specific first-person viewpoint, buyer stakes, concrete questions and a clear description of the service. It retains meaningful limits and avoids fabricated founder memories, quotes, client examples, revenue or reach. The founder must approve the first-person opinions and service wording.

**Personal-story gap:** the project supplies an actual research exercise but does not provide a specific, owner-confirmed personal anecdote. Post 02 uses the documented project research as the real experience. The other first-person drafts express proposed viewpoints or methods; they must not be presented as verified past client stories. Add an actual founder decision, context, consequence and approved details when supplied.

## Claims and source map

- **S1 / Outspoken reference sample:** 279 selected posts from 61 accounts; 276 observed reaction counts with a median of 107.5; 256 observed comment counts with a median of 36.5. Counters captured 20–22 October 2026. The two medians have different denominators. Reactions combine reaction types, comments are separate, and reach, clicks and conversion are not observed. The sample is selected, combines different accounts and post ages and cannot establish causal format effects or a platform benchmark. [Published explanatory report](https://github.com/mcharniuk1-spec/linkedin/tree/main/reports/20261105). Used in posts 02 and 09.
- **S2 / Survey:** LinkedIn reports that 73% of decision-makers surveyed in the 2024 Edelman–LinkedIn B2B Thought Leadership research viewed an organisation's thought leadership as a more trustworthy basis for evaluating its capabilities than marketing materials and product sheets. This is a survey response; it does not establish that any post causes a sale or that Outspoken achieved client outcomes. [LinkedIn, 4 March 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/trends-tips/this-week-in-marketing-make-an-impact-with-thought-leadership). Used in post 05.
- **S3 / Offer and process:** Owner-approved value proposition and service narrative in `writing-system/START-HERE.md` and `DESIGN.md`. The process is the proposed offer, not a claim that a named client has already received it. Posts 01, 03, 04, 06–08 and 10 present an editorial viewpoint or process derived from this brief.

## Visual provenance and review

Each of ten posts has a graphic and a people-led image. The graphic direction incorporates existing R15 Outspoken visual elements; people-led images use ten newly generated fictional scenes. The latter are marked “illustrative scene” and do not depict real founders, customers or documented client work. The R13 identity and licensed project fonts are used. Layout source is `render.js` and the editable text is `content.json`; SVG render inputs retain the layered composition. Owner acceptance, factual approval of the service, and independent creative review remain open.
