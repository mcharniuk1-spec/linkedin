A reaction is a visible response such as Like, Celebrate or Insightful. A comment is a separate reply. They are different measures.

In our selected sample of 279 posts, reactions were available for 276 posts. The median was 107.5 reactions. Comments were available for 256 posts; their median was 36.5. Captures took place 20–22 October 2026.

This is a selected reference sample, not a current platform benchmark. We did not observe reach, clicks or conversions, so these figures cannot establish an engagement rate or sales result.
