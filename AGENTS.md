## Research invariants

1. Preserve exact publication identity, original observation time, source hash and parser provenance. A parser replay is a derivative of old evidence, not a new capture. Two compatible-parser observations at least6h apart are required for velocity; never lower that floor to manufacture a result.
2. Keep separate fields for reference-role fit, content meaning/quality, observed performance, change, prospect firmographics, opportunity hypotheses, expressed need and buying interest. Reactions do not prove reach, impressions, causality, buyer quality or sales.
3. Search ranking/date hints and account lists are discovery seeds. Validate actual target identity and source publication date. Missing/conflicting/future dates stay holds. Deduplicate across the explicit corpus; preserve real publication variants but avoid treating repeated wording as independent patterns.
4. Use bounded anonymous public access with source receipts, caps, redirect limits, overlap lock and stop handling. No login/cookies/proxies/auth bypass or paid fallback follows from this contract. Observe each backend's actual access failures and do not retry its blocked resources through another route.
5. Confirm professional reference relevance before acquiring a new author's media. Keep unresolved identity/role matches as holds. Do not build prospect employee dossiers or full post/media histories; prospect summaries stay separate.
6. Originals and alternative transcriptions remain separate. Cached local Whisper can supply machine comparisons; no model downloads by default. Mechanical QA, ASR agreement and sampled frames do not prove listening, accuracy or continuous watching. Clear source-bound audio review only when the exact audio was actually reviewed; otherwise retain the gap.
7. Describe actual images and timestamped video evidence, their story/style/claim relation and limitations. Explain creative mechanisms as hypotheses. Public availability does not establish media reuse rights.
8. No recurring run, cron, background service, wake adapter, outreach, publishing, advertising spend or website deployment is activated by this repository. The current continuation expressly keeps recurring collection off. A one-off active-task wait for due observations is not a recurring service.
