## The first useful tool views

These are proposed views over the existing modules, not ten new built products. Voice + Pipeline includes the Voice foundation; the table shows the main use.

|---|---|---|---|

## What the customer needs

For the initial writing workflow: a product explanation, relevant examples, approved factual sources, rough voice input and a reviewer. Use a shared document for sources and suggestions, a Sheet/board for planning, native LinkedIn for preview/publishing and available analytics, and a CRM or Sheet for actual conversations. A new paid client subscription is not inherently required for this starting workflow; selected optional features may require one. Account access, operator and payer must be explicit.

Monid would be an optional agency research input. The client should receive a readable shortlist and reasoning, not be required to operate provider APIs. Review source rights, privacy, export, retention, access and actual cost before offering it to clients.

## Human explanation of the packages — draft for review

“Many founders can explain their product well in a conversation, but turning that knowledge into useful LinkedIn content takes a different kind of work. That’s why Voice starts with your product, buyers and point of view. We use that material to clarify the profile, plan the topics, write the posts and create visuals that help someone understand the idea. You check the facts and the position. The practical result is a clearer profile and a repeatable way to explain what your company does.

Voice + Pipeline adds the work around starting and continuing relevant buyer conversations: finding suitable companies and people, checking why they fit, preparing personal messages, working with actual replies and carrying the context into a meeting brief. It builds on the same content foundation. We agree the scope and responsibilities before outreach starts.”

This wording describes the service work and deliverables. It does not promise reach, leads or sales. Final fees and included third-party tools depend on the service/tool/cost decisions; the existing $800/$2,500 figures remain hypotheses. This draft was not applied to the website or gallery.
