## 1. OUTSP-14 — Give the buyer something useful without a purchase

**Goal:** A founder should leave the introduction with a useful next action, even if they do not buy.

1. Choose the output: **A — useful observations during the call; B — a one-page note; C — one post outline.** Recommend B because the founder can use and share it afterward.
2. Check the proposed one-page contents: up to three observations, each paired with an action and a way to check the result; two content directions based on material the founder supplies; one example opening; one next step.
3. Set eligibility. Recommend the note for a relevant founder who attends the call and supplies enough material, whether or not they buy. For missing material or a poor fit, give a useful live explanation and a clear next step.
4. Confirm the boundary. This note covers a quick assessment and initial direction. Full strategy, ongoing writing, production and implementation belong in the paid service.

**Illustrative example — not a customer audit:** A reporting-product founder supplies a profile headline, one product walkthrough and a recurring buyer question. The note could show how to replace an unclear headline, identify where the walkthrough needs an example, and suggest one buyer-focused Featured item. Two post directions could explain a product choice and show how a user checks an answer. An example opening: “Before connecting company data to an AI reporting tool, try one report with sample data.” Each recommendation must be justified against the actual supplied material before delivery.

**Answer:** A, B or C; and whether the proposed eligibility is suitable.
