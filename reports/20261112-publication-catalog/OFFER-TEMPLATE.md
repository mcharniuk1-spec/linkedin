## Choose the service foundation

### Voice

Strategy, profile and recurring content work built from the founder's or team's actual knowledge. The scoped work can include buyer and message direction, profile recommendations or copy, content planning, original posts, meaningful visuals, factual and voice review, publishing responsibilities and reporting.

### Voice + Pipeline

Includes the agreed Voice foundation. Adds company and contact research, source and fit checks, outreach planning, agreed campaign operation, reply qualification, meeting context and pipeline reporting. The ICP belongs to your business and requires your review. Contact data, account access and sending each need a defined owner and approval.

### Optional email support

Email finding or verification and campaign/reply planning can be included only when they solve an agreed need. Finding a candidate contact is separate from sending. Sending uses an authorized client-owned mailbox and agreed recipient, review and stop rules. Tool access and costs are checked before use.

### Optional LinkedIn ads

Ads strategy or campaign management is a separate option. Define the objective, audience, creative, landing path, account roles and measurement before proposing implementation. Media fees are separate from agency fees. No spend or account access is assumed.
