## Eight writing and visualization lessons

### H01 · Define popularity relative to its evidence and cohort

Eight saved strong-relative labels come from four author/format/age/language cohorts. Their observed reactions range from 53 to 5,518; an ordinary-reference post has 1,904. Absolute counter size and relative standing answer different questions.

Use the reader job and evidence packet to choose a structure. Describe any counter as a dated observation, never as viral reach, buyer quality or proof of the technique.

**Limit/counterexample:** Creator audience, publication age, topic, distribution and promotion can explain differences;245 of 279 posts lack enough comparable source records.

### H02 · Show a professional decision rather than manufacture vulnerability

The two high relative image-story references use personal before/after situations, while another same-cohort story is ordinary relative to its author. The functional transfer is an intelligible choice and reflection, not a guaranteed story effect.

Recover the actual goal, constraint, rejected alternative, choice, consequence and interpretation. Without a confirmed event, write a proposed method or labelled example.

**Limit/counterexample:** A strong author network and an older post can generate large counters independently of story structure. First-person account and commercial results remain source-reported.

### H03 · Make one useful routine or mechanism inspectable

Source interpretations contrast concrete meeting agreements, clarifying questions and role responsibilities with a small abstract forecast. The observed counts do not isolate which component mattered.

Show a sequence with a real input, decision, output and owner; explain why each transition matters. Keep metaphors secondary to the literal workflow.

**Limit/counterexample:** The low-relative listening routine is still practically useful. A named proprietary concept and a strong-relative label do not verify its persuasion claims.

### H04 · Choose a visual that explains the same relationship as the argument

Saved prior assessments describe diagnostic tables, a four-question graphic and a layered positioning equation. Their exact fine print was not fully audited; this review did not reopen source images.

Use a checklist for missing information, a path for a workflow, a table for alternatives, or a sourced chart for a measured comparison. Repeat the literal claim and limitation in accessible text.

**Limit/counterexample:** The report labels only 13 framework/diagram posts from four authors. It is not an independent test of diagrams, and the source examples include text-led methods too.

### H05 · Connect evidence to a bounded consequence

A strong-relative reframe example includes unsupported prescriptions; a low-relative everyday analogy remains a recognizable explanatory device. Neither supplies causal business evidence.

State what the observation supports, explain the reasoning and name the condition that would change the conclusion. A hypothetical workflow can explain a method but cannot establish product performance.

**Limit/counterexample:** Concrete detail, a citation or a precise statistic can still be irrelevant, selected or unverified. Do not add old survey percentages or implied savings merely to make a panel persuasive.

### H06 · Use humor for recognition when it fits the author and stakes

A saved dialogue-humor interpretation uses a recognizable communication difficulty. The adjacent-humor topic contains only six selected posts; an entertain job is a different label and also has six.

A small reversal or plainly illustrative scene may add interest. Keep data, product limitations, privacy and fictional-person labels literal. Humor is optional and needs author voice review.

**Limit/counterexample:** These categories do not establish a humor uplift or show that forced jokes improve trust. Fictional dialogue cannot become a claimed founder or customer event.

### H07 · Do not turn format or account medians into a universal rule

Aggregate image reactions have a higher median than text in the selected report, but within-author image/text comparisons split six positive and five negative. These comparisons have only two or more observations per format and do not hold age/topic/promotion constant.

Select the medium for the explanation. Give a founder account a confirmed decision and a company account a distinct process or proof artifact instead of duplicating both or promising better reach.

**Limit/counterexample:** April Dunford image versus text medians 560 versus 738 reverse the aggregate; Chris Orlob image 32.5 versus text 41.5 does as well. No account type, carousel length, font or image style is causally preferred by this corpus.

### H08 · Let the ending serve the job rather than a template

The saved corpus has 63 offer announcements, 91 method posts and six discussion posts; observed reaction medians do not reveal conversion. One strong-relative source explicitly refuses a blanket engagement-based length recommendation.

Close a diagnosis with a usable check, a research observation with its conclusion, a real uncertainty with one answerable question, and an offer with its approved next step.

**Limit/counterexample:** A self-selected reply is not a representative buyer survey. A low reaction median does not justify banning offers or replacing Book an intro call with a comment request.

## Apply to all ten current structures

| Concept | Writing change / retained boundary | Visualization job |
|---|---|---|
| 01-the-reason | Show why one product choice matters to a buyer. Keep an illustrative editorial observation until the author confirms a real decision. | Use one product→choice→buyer relationship; no fictional portrait may imply a testimonial. |
| 02-what-we-actually-saw | Lead with the bounded question and the 279-post denominator; place 276 reaction / 256 comment observations beside their medians and state 22 October 2026. | A small source-labelled count/coverage table, not a viral leaderboard. |
| 03-the-interview | Ask why the rejected alternative was plausible, what the author knew then and what changed. Keep process language proposed. | A question→source note→draft→author check path, with any dialogue explicitly illustrative. |
| 04-positioning | Make all four promised checks visible: buyer, task, alternative and supported difference. Label the rewrite hypothetical. | Four-check diagnostic with readable labels and a single concrete before/after sentence. |
| 05-proof | Separate an asserted capability, a one-off demonstration and a repeated measurement; explain what each can support. | A literal evidence ladder or claim→test→limit table. Do not retain the omitted 73% figure in old artwork. |
| 06-product-to-buyer | Follow trigger→input→output→human review → next decision. Retain technical detail that changes evaluation. | One consistent workflow diagram whose arrows mean sequence, not measured improvement. |
| 07-two-voices | Allocate different contributions to one buyer question. Do not claim founder accounts causally outperform company accounts. | Two role columns sharing one question; avoid mirrored copy and invented identity proof. |
| 08-visuals | Ask what relationship the image helps the reader assess; distinguish explanation, demonstration and illustration. | Choose the representation by job. Keep fictional-scene disclosures and source/limit labels readable at mobile scale. |
| 09-measure | Make this a decision-to-measure diagnostic instead of repeating post02. Unknown buyer intent and missing impressions stay unknown. | Question→availablemeasure→unanswereddecision map, with null clearly distinguished from observed zero. |
| 10-the-delivery | State fit, founder input, proposed output, review responsibility and the exact approved CTA. Make no reach or sales guarantee. | A concise service/output handoff; avoid depicting illustrative scenes as customers or finished results. |

Keep the current ten distinct reader questions. Post 02 is the selected-sample observation; post 09 is the question-to-measure diagnosis. Post 03 remains a proposed interview method until an actual episode is confirmed. Post 05 does not carry the old 73% statistic into new artwork. Post 10 retains **Book an intro call** and needs operational scope confirmation. A repeated design family is acceptable when it improves recognition; a repeated diagram that adds no meaning is not evidence-driven design.
