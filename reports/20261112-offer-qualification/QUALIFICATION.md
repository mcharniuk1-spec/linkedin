# OUTSP-15: proposed customer qualification rules

Keep the dimensions separate. A combined lead score would hide important gaps.

| Dimension | Proceed | Clarify or hold | Decline or redirect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company fit | A working B2B AI SaaS product, Europe focus and an identifiable business buyer. | An adjacent market or size/stage outside the preferred filter. | An unrelated request with no suitable service route. |
| Stated need | The founder identifies a current profile, content or buyer-conversation problem. | A general growth wish without an example. | The request requires guaranteed reach, leads or invented proof. |
| Source material | A usable product explanation, decision, example or founder position. | Missing facts, proof or disclosure permission. | The content must fabricate results or disclose material without permission. |
| Delivery readiness | A named factual approver and sustainable source/review capacity. | The approver, timing or responsibilities are unknown. | No feasible way to supply or approve factual content. |
| Access | An authorized account workflow or a delivery-only service. | Exact roles and permissions remain undefined. | The required method bypasses access restrictions. |
| Commercial next step | Explicit interest in scope, price or another defined step. | Interest is unknown; attendance and likes do not establish it. | The person declines. Record that decision and stop the relevant follow-up. |

The preferred 10–50 staff and Seed–Series B criteria are research defaults. They are not independent evidence that another founder is unsuitable. Record budget, decision authority and buying interest separately. Set price only after the scope, tools and timed costing are complete.

**Output:** A short qualification record with source/date, stated problem, separate dispositions for fit, need, evidence, readiness and interest, an approved source list, cadence rationale, missing inputs, next action and owner. Task 42 must still build and verify the actual 50–80-company list. These rules do not create verified prospects.
