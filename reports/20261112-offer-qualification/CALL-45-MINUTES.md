# The 45-minute discovery and qualification call

The call should produce a clear fit decision, a useful next action and a provisional publishing plan. A written free output depends on the owner's choice in OUTSP-14/84.

| Minutes | Decision | Questions and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Purpose | What should this conversation help you decide? Confirm note-taking and scope. |
| 3–10 | Product and company | What do you offer? Who uses it? Who approves a purchase? Which public explanation or demo can we inspect? |
| 10–19 | Buyer problem | What changed? Which buyer question or objection keeps appearing? What actual statement or behaviour supports it? |
| 19–27 | Experience and voice | Describe one situation: goal, constraint, alternatives, choice, reason, action and observed consequence. Which parts are verified? What may be public? |
| 27–34 | Account baseline | Personal profile or company Page? What material and analytics are available? Separate distribution, replies, qualified conversations and sales. |
| 34–39 | Cadence and delivery | How many distinct usable examples exist? Who reviews? How much time can they give? Agree an initial number of posts, formats, owner and review date. |
| 39–43 | Fit and next step | Record company fit, stated need, evidence, feasibility and buying interest separately. Agree the next action. |
| 43–45 | Readback | Repeat facts, proposals, unknowns, cadence, inputs, owner and date. Ask for corrections. |

## Ask at the right depth

- **Monthly strategy:** Which buyer decisions matter? What is the commercial purpose? What source material and author positions are available? What do we want to learn?
- **Weekly plan:** Which real questions and examples will we use? Which format explains each one? Who reviews and what should the reader do next?
- **Individual post:** Ask only for the missing fact, decision, example or stance that changes that draft.

For a personal story, ask what happened, the author's role, what they knew then, the alternative, their choice and reason, their action, the observed consequence and the lesson. Record source and permission. If the consequence is unknown, keep it unknown. A professional recommendation or labelled illustration can be useful without an autobiographical episode.

One post per week or a test of two to three are planning options. A four-to-six-week review window is also a proposal, not a finding from the historical sample. Before increasing output, review source quality, approval delays, workload and relevant buyer conversations.
