# What the saved LinkedIn data supports

The frozen corpus contains **279 posts, 61 accounts and 477 snapshots**. The 198 reobservations are additional snapshots of existing posts. They are not new publications. Five source publication dates are missing or invalid. All 279 post IDs join to the 61 account URLs.

At the publication-row level, the parser records 238 Person, 36 Organization and five unknown author types. This does not identify founder, executive or creator subroles. The source hash and checks are retained in the local QA record. Original third-party post text and media are not republished here.

The sample is selected, historical and incomplete for individual author timelines. Its post counts and observed gaps cannot establish true posting frequency, an optimal cadence, reach, conversion or a current market trend. Public reactions do not supply those missing outcomes.

| Account and purpose | Useful source material | What informs cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Founder or executive personal profile | Actual choices, buyer objections, product examples and point of view. | Founder source supply, review time and buyer understanding. |
| Practitioner or expert | Methods, worked examples, troubleshooting and limitations. | Distinct teachable material and capacity to answer follow-up questions. |
| Creator or media-led account | Recurring analysis, interviews or editorial series. | Research capacity, editorial capacity and a sustained audience purpose. |
| Company Page | Product updates, team expertise, customer-approved evidence and buying-group questions. | Business purpose, approval owner and its relationship to founder content. |

These are discovery categories. They are not verified classifications of all 61 accounts. Role labels require identity evidence. A personal account may serve more than one purpose.

**Planning options:** Start with one well-supported post per week when source or review capacity is limited. Test two to three when distinct material and capacity support it. Higher frequency needs its own justification. These are hypotheses, not performance prescriptions. Agree a trial and review point during the 45-minute call. Then inspect actual effort and buyer questions before changing the plan.
