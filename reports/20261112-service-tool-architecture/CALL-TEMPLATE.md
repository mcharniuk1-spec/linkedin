# 45-minute discovery call

For working B2B businesses changing operations with AI or seeking customers, small digital agencies, growth firms, and Series A/B startups. AI SaaS is eligible, not exclusive. Use intake to prioritize live questions.

## Before the call: intake (5–8 minutes, async)

Ask for company/role, offer/customer, goal/obstacle, prior attempts/results, channels/tools, capacity/decision process, timing, one safe example artifact, and data/access/budget constraints. Mark gaps. No credentials or sensitive customer data.

## Live call: 45 minutes

**0–4 | Frame.** Explain goal, process, evidence, constraints, and next-step recommendation. No assumed cadence or package. Confirm notes; recording needs separate permission.

**4–12 | Goal.** What do you sell, to whom, and how do customers buy? Which segment matters now? Why now? What result would make 90 days successful, and how will you measure it?

**12–22 | Current state.** Where do opportunities or gains come from? Walk through one win and one stalled case. What is the process? Which metrics are observed, for what period/denominator/source? What is missing or estimated?

**22–31 | Constraint.** Where does work fail (positioning, offer, demand, conversion, capacity, handoffs, data, follow-up)? What did tests cost and achieve? What must not change? Who owns work; what review capacity exists?

**31–38 | Select relevant branch(es), only as needed.**

- **Content / LinkedIn:** audience, offer, roles, source supply, approvals, current cadence and rationale. Choose cadence from role, supply, and review capacity.
- **Outbound / email:** target, approved data basis, sequence/deliverability evidence, reply handling, owner capacity. Do not promise lists, sending, or access.
- **Paid ads:** objective, channel, evidenced spend/results, landing path, tracking, constraints, budget authority. Do not imply spend or campaign changes are included.
- **AI / agent integration:** task, inputs/outputs, systems/access owner, failure cost, human review, privacy limits, success measure. Consider bounded configuration/template or verified integration; do not presume custom software.

Ask only relevant branches. If several apply, prioritize one and defer the rest.

**38–43 | Feasibility.** Who decides and does the work? What approvals/dependencies apply? What support level is useful? What timing and investment range are realistic? Any procurement or security review?

**43–45 | Confirm.** Reflect goal, evidence, constraint, gaps, and next step. Ask what is wrong or missing. Confirm safe use of materials. Uncosted scope, price, timing, or result is not accepted.

## Capture and route

Capture source, period/denominator, confidence (observed/reported/estimated/unknown), owner, constraint, follow-up. Map to `field-map.json`. Free report: diagnosis, priorities, execution blocks, deliverables, measures, dependencies, gaps—not implementation steps. Offer brief strategy and/or **one** bounded adapted tool (configuration, templates, verified integration). Set terms after costing. No assumed custom build, free spend, access, or outcome promise.

## Intake and branch completeness

Use call-field-map.json for all 22 field groups. Prefill markets/languages, buyer alternatives, current tools/owners/payers and safe sample material before the call where available. Clarify what counts as a suitable company/contact/conversation/meeting. For a tool, capture the actual agent/interface and a sample acceptance check. At closing, record whether the relevant free route is a brief strategy, one adapted tool or both; exact scope/period waits for the agency comparison and costing. Missing data is a follow-up, not a reason to invent an answer or extend every branch beyond 45 minutes.

Operator note: prefill known facts before the call. Treat the 22 groups as a coverage map, not a 22-question script. Ask only the missing item that changes the selected recommendation; defer dense tool-acceptance or competitor detail to the relevant branch or follow-up.
