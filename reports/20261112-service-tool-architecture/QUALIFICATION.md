# Working B2B customers: qualification v2 — OUTSP-15

Do not restrict research eligibility to AI SaaS. Start with an operating B2B offer and a real customer acquisition/communication task.

| Research cohort | Why it may be relevant | What still needs evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Small B2B services and digital agencies | Expertise may be strong while positioning, founder content or customer development is inconsistent. | Actual offer, buyer, capacity, current gap and willingness to work on it. |
| Firms adapting services through AI | A changed offer may need a clearer explanation and a new customer segment. | What changed, who benefits and what can actually be delivered. |
| Growing B2B firms and Series A/B startups | Expansion may create a need for repeatable content, new accounts or coordinated channels. | Growth stage/funding is not proof of need, authority or buying interest. |
| Existing B2B AI SaaS cohort | Retains continuity with our prior offer and sources. | Qualify individually; historical LinkedIn observations do not prove commercial fit. |

These are approved research directions, not validated demand segments. Select one or two first test cohorts after Monday rather than treating every business as a prospect. Geography, language, budget, delivery capacity and source permissions remain explicit filters; old size/stage preferences are not universal gates.

Record separately: company/offer fit; expressed need; supporting facts and source dates; delivery readiness; account/access route; buying interest; decision process; budget/timing; next action. Require a working offer, an identifiable B2B buyer/problem, usable source material and a named reviewer. Missing readiness can mean a hold rather than rejection. Delivery-only work remains possible without account access. The client's customers require their own approved ICP; do not copy OutSpoken's ICP into a client campaign.

A suitable company with no expressed need remains a research candidate. A client who cannot supply facts or review drafts needs a preparation step. A request for guaranteed outcomes or invented proof does not fit the operating method. The 45-minute call selects provisional cadence and review arrangements from the actual material and capacity.
