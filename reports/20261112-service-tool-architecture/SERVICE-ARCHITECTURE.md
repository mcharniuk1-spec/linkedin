# A complete service, with tools selected by the work

The client buys clearer positioning/content and, where agreed, a structured way to find and develop relevant B2B conversations. A generator or data subscription supports one part of that work. It does not replace strategy, factual review or delivery ownership.

Diagnose and propose | Compare the current situation with the buyer's task and practical delivery limits. | Brief pre-flight strategy: priorities, blocks, tools, expected outputs, dependencies and personalized offer; one adapted tool where suitable. | Agency proposes; client corrects. ## How the offers fit

**Voice** starts with the founder's or business team's actual knowledge. It covers the agreed strategy, profile, content planning, writing, meaningful visual production, review and publishing/engagement responsibilities. Tools help the team explain the offer and work consistently. Content volume, channels and operator are scoped for that client.

**Voice + Pipeline** builds on that foundation. It adds company/person research, source checks, relevant outreach planning, agreed channel operation, reply qualification, meeting briefs and pipeline reporting. Email finding and campaign tools are selected when they solve a real need. Ads strategy/management is a separate option until the owner selects its package treatment; it is not silently included.

For each client, connect the same buyer problem across profile, content, message, useful resource and sales conversation. Record actual next steps, not a blended engagement/lead score. The outcome we can commit to is the specified work and deliverables; reach, replies, meetings and revenue need observed baselines and cannot be guaranteed from tool use.
