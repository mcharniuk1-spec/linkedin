## What the research changes in our work

1. Start with the reader’s question. Teaching a method is common in this selected corpus, but frequency is not proof of effectiveness.
2. Show a real choice, concrete example or useful distinction. A personal story is one available pattern; do not manufacture one for every post.
3. Put the reason between evidence and advice. A number does not explain its own business consequence.
4. Match the visual to the question. Image/text comparisons in this sample are confounded and do not prove an image advantage.
5. Treat early publications as a learning cycle. Separate comprehension, audience evidence, relevant conversations and revenue.

These are source-informed editorial choices to evaluate on our own work. They are not algorithm rules, guaranteed hooks or sales forecasts.
