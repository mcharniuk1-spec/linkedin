Images have a higher median than text in this selected sample. Authors, subjects, post ages and distribution differ; format was not assigned. That makes an “images improve results” conclusion unsupported. We choose the visual for its explanatory job, then evaluate our own posts. Nine document posts are too limited to establish a general carousel rule.

## Practical decisions, with their limits

| Evidence / method | Use in our work | What remains a hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
|91 posts teach a method;36 tell stories.|Use useful teaching and truthful decision stories as different patterns.|Neither frequency nor observed medians establish which will work for Max.|
|Only8 repost counts are visible.|Keep the company repost as a separate useful communication unit.|No reliable benchmark for repost volume, timing or benefit.|
|245 posts lack enough comparable records.|Read actual reader questions and source details before ranking examples.|Do not sell a viral scoring model from this sample.|
|Media review modalities differ.|Use existing descriptive analysis with its source and modality labels.|No full-video/audio or complete fresh visual review is implied.|
|Reference accounts are not the client’s author.|Transfer a function: diagnostic question, fair comparison, concrete example.|Do not copy a creator’s story, signature voice or promised results.|

## How to review the reader

1. Open[index.html](index.html). Search a buyer topic or author and filter by editorial purpose.
2. Open a row’s saved source locator or inspect its ID in the[full derived dataset](../../datasets/current/dataset.json).
3. Write the reader question, point, evidence and useful limit in your own words.
4. Choose one adopt/adapt/reject decision; state what our own post would need.
5. Compare a relevant low-count example as well as a high-count example.

The reader shows every one of the279 derived records. It does not contain raw creator posts, original media or full transcripts. Its counter fields preserve nulls as unknown.
