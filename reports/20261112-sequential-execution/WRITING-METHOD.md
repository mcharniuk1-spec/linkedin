# Writing as Max, then adapting for OutSpoken

## The source packet

Before drafting, identify the author, intended reader, actual decision, known facts, permitted public detail, supported stance, uncertainty and next step. Use the client’s own buyer/voice packet in client work. Our reference accounts and the OutSpoken agency ICP cannot supply someone else’s experience or audience.

The current Max lens is provisional: direct, practical, evidence-aware, interested in how work actually happens, and willing to explain trade-offs. It comes from the owner’s current instructions, a bounded historical original Max post and the existing provisional voice guide. This is not a measured personality profile. ArchFlow contributes a habit of keeping source, rationale, responsibility and next action visible; its company positioning and technical vocabulary do not become OutSpoken’s offer or visual identity.

## Repeatable drafting sequence

| Step | Work | Expected output / check |
|---|---|---|
|1|Write “After this post, the reader can better…”|One reader decision, not just a topic.|
|2|List the claim, source, reasoning and limit.|A supported point before a dramatic opening.|
|3|Select a genre: real decision story, interpretation, explainer, review, method or offer.|Do not force an anecdote onto a statistics post.|
|4|Draft two openings from the same facts.|Each promises something the body actually delivers.|
|5|Give each paragraph a role.|Scene/question → evidence/example → interpretation → useful consequence, where the material needs them. Paragraph breaks follow meaning; do not split mechanically after every sentence.|
|6|End with the consequence, a real question or a relevant offer.|An earned ending; no compulsory engagement request. Preserve “Book an intro call” when used.|
|7|Edit in Max’s voice, with natural rhythm and room to read.|Keep his stance and supported emotion; no invented frustration, victory, conversation or certainty.|
|8|Design the explanation.|Choose an annotation, comparison, coverage chart, practical example or relevant context image. A visual earns its place by helping the reader.|
|9|Prepare a separate company repost introduction.|Add a method, implication or useful prompt. Do not simply duplicate the personal caption.|
|10|Check facts, voice and visual meaning separately.|Author accepts his stance; the reviewer checks the sources; visual text matches the current caption revision.|

## Story architecture and evidence

For an actual decision story: situation → goal and constraint → real alternatives → choice → observed consequence → present interpretation → useful boundary. If there is no observed consequence, say what was decided and what is still being tested. Do not create a result to finish the arc.

For research: question → source/method → result with denominator/date → what it may mean → alternative explanation → practical next check. For a product explanation: reader’s task → concrete interaction → person and handoff → relevant difference → evidence needed. For an offer: who/when it fits → work included → client input/review → delivered artifact → limits → next step.

**Before:** “Our evidence architecture preserves observation limits.”

**After, proposed for Max:** “Our LinkedIn sample has a reaction median of107.5. I would not use it as a target for my next post.” The body explains the selected sample,276 observed reaction counts and unknown reach/sales. The hook and payoff use the same evidence.

**Before:** “Input → AI → Impact.”

**After, illustrative:** “A support lead reviews unclear requests before a reply goes out.” Annotate the person, action and control point; check the real product before using that line as a product claim.

Emotion comes from an actual stake or an author-endorsed view. Curiosity can be specific: “Which case would you need to see before trusting this workflow?” Humor can be restrained self-observation or a precise detail; it is optional. A vivid phrase does not strengthen the evidence.

## Supplied methodology integration

Foundations/genre routing separate subject, reader task and medium. Claim types and Toulmin-informed reasoning connect evidence to the conclusion without forcing formal labels into the caption. Narrative methods preserve what the author knew then versus reflection now. Paragraph/reverse-outline methods give each unit a contribution. Agent/evaluation methods freeze facts and separate making from review. LinkedIn chapter14 contributes professional usefulness, functional openings, meaningful spacing, truthful stories, relevant response prompts and measurement limits.

The independent method review found these safeguards already present in the current skills. Retain the baseline. Add the paired founder/company brief at task level; do not rewrite/install bundles merely to create the appearance of progress. If real behavioral failures later justify a change, stage the smallest candidate, test on frozen inputs, then scan/install the exact changed bundle before activation.
