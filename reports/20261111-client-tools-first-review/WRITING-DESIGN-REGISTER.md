## Method/source integration contract

The supplied corpus provides:01 foundations and genre routing;02 claim types, evidence/reasoning, real alternatives and professional stories;03 paragraph/narrative/review workflows and frozen fact packets;04 bounded agent roles/evaluation;14 LinkedIn reader usefulness, functional openings, truthful experience and observation limits. The existing [adoption matrix](../../docs/linkedin-writing/METHOD-INTEGRATION.md) and [identity record](../../docs/linkedin-writing/supplied-source-manifest.json) preserve the prior thirteen-file review. This turn reread the LinkedIn chapter; it did not render every supplied PDF/DOCX or reread all cited books.

Every writing tool must carry the buyer/reader brief, source IDs, actual author viewpoint, claim type, numerical context, reasoning link, relevant limit, paragraph function and review disposition through each stage. Select a truthful story/argument structure; do not force every post into an anecdote. Draft the supported point first, then choose a hook it actually pays off. Humor remains optional and must not invent an event or person. End with a useful conclusion or a question only when the answer is genuinely needed.

The full saved279-post/61-account corpus is a dated reference set. The reused [evidence checks](evidence-reuse-check.json) confirm its exact blob/counts and descriptive medians. It supplies traceable construction hypotheses and eligibility/coverage metadata—not copied creator prose, client facts/voice, current market demand, representative conversion or a viral score. No source media was newly watched in this review.
