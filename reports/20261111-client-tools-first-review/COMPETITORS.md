## Practical positioning for OutSpoken

The clearest service gap to own is **editorial rigor made visible**: a buyer brief, source/claim map, voice calibration, draft rationale, client approval and a separate outcome review. Competitors commonly promise voice, consistency, strategy and growth; the prior audit does not establish that any provider's process causes its displayed outcomes.

Use transparent packages to explain the work, not to imitate a competitor's price. Distinguish service from software and outbound: OutSpoken's differentiator should be the quality and traceability of the client's point of view, not a claim that publishing alone generates qualified pipeline. Keep proof labels explicit: observed client activity, attributed case outcome, testimonial, hypothesis, or independently verified result.
