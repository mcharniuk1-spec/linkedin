## Writing workflow

1. Define the reader, buying situation, decision and post/page job. Use evidence and actual buyer language; do not infer buying interest from low LinkedIn activity.
2. Assemble an approved source pack: source IDs/dates/rights, exact facts and quotes, founder point of view, product limitations, proof strength and unresolved claims. Preserve source originals.
3. Prepare a voice brief before drafting: vocabulary, sentence rhythm, viewpoint, degree of detail, opinions, examples, exclusions and exact protected wording. Use permissioned founder examples rather than imitating another creator.
4. Select the content category and editorial job using the research category guide. A topic, creative format, reference value and observed performance are different fields. A resource giveaway or strong hook is not proof of commercial value.
5. Draft one clear buyer-relevant argument: problem/context → specific experience/evidence → implication/decision → useful next step. Do not invent customer results, screenshots, interviews, scarcity or personal experiences.
6. Edit for natural voice, specificity and readability. Then reconcile every factual claim/number/quote/CTA with its source. Humanizing must preserve facts, intent and useful technical detail.
7. For a website page, add the dated search-intent brief, page purpose/URL, title/meta, heading hierarchy, canonical/internal links, descriptive asset text and truthful structured data. Make useful direct answers easy to locate. Ensure ad→page promise continuity and clear next action. Never stuff keywords or make invisible search-only claims.
8. Review copy quality, evidence and conversion hypothesis; validate the pack, then inspect actual desktop/mobile layout, long text, accessibility and reduced-motion behavior. A validator proves structure, not persuasion, search ranking or customer demand.
9. Bind the exact copy revision/hash to components, stories and native-design mapping. Send any changed slot back through source/voice/search review. Keep text editable.
10. Measure only actual authorized outcomes. Search visibility, AI citations, engagement and sales remain separate measured results; do not promise them from skill use.

LinkedIn publication text should answer a relevant reader problem and fit the actual format. Website technical SEO is a separate task; search hints, hashtags or wording cannot establish LinkedIn reach. Research patterns inspire original work, not copying creator text or uncleared media.

## Content-pack fields

Record: client/agency scope; reader and buying situation; page/post ID; category/job/format; source and claim IDs; exact approved copy/slots; voice and natural-language review; metadata/URL/link/schema fields; asset IDs/alt/caption; rights state; copy revision/hash; reviewer/time; gaps and owner acceptance state. Keep facts, interpretations, hypotheses and missing evidence distinct.
