## S01 — Founder positioning and buyer strategy

Give buyers a clear reason to pay attention. We turn your market, product and point of view into a position your LinkedIn content can build on.

**Buyer problem:** Technical expertise and product progress do not yet form a clear, buyer-relevant story.
**Cadence:** Initial sprint; quarterly review or after a material product/ICP change

**Tasks**

- Audit founder/company presence and approved source documents
- Define buyer roles, buying committee and jobs to be done
- Separate company positioning from the founder’s editorial point of view
- Map alternatives, competitor claims and credible differentiation
- Interview the founder on decisions, trade-offs, customer problems and business milestones
- Build message hierarchy, objections, editorial pillars and conversion paths
- Set baseline metrics and a 90-day test plan

**Deliverables**

- ICP and buying-committee map
- Positioning and messaging brief
- Founder narrative and voice direction
- Competitor claim map
- 90-day editorial and measurement plan

**Acceptance**

- Every business claim links to an approved source or is labeled hypothesis
- ICP, buying trigger, alternative and reason to believe are explicit
- Founder approves exact voice and message brief
- Do not infer demand, marketing capacity or budget from company size or funding

**Measures**

- Target-founder comprehension feedback
- Buyer relevance of agreed topics
- Qualified conversation baseline

**Dependencies**

- Founder availability
- Product and customer evidence approved for use

**Boundaries**

- Positioning is a hypothesis until tested with real buyers
- No brand selection, name clearance or outreach is implied

**Skills:** product-marketing, market-research-competitive-intelligence, company-research, content-strategy, customer-journey-map, decision-memo-builder

**Tools:** Codex native agents, Public web search, agent-browser, Approved client documents, Local Markdown/JSON

## S02 — Founder knowledge and voice system

Build content from what you actually know. We organize your interviews, product knowledge and customer evidence so every draft stays useful, accurate and recognizably yours.

**Buyer problem:** Useful knowledge is scattered across documents, calls and the founder’s head; generic prompts lose context.
**Cadence:** Onboarding; weekly source intake; monthly cleanup

**Tasks**

- Register each source with owner, rights, version, hash and permitted audience
- Extract PDFs, documents and spreadsheets without replacing originals
- Transcribe approved recordings and preserve the original language
- Separate product facts, founder opinions, customer quotations and untested ideas
- Create a voice guide from approved samples and banned phrasing
- Maintain a product glossary, claim ledger, story bank and change log
- Retrieve by exact source/section first and attach evidence to drafts
- Review updates after product releases and customer interviews

**Deliverables**

- Source register and approved corpus allowlist
- Founder voice guide and examples
- Product/ICP glossary
- Claim ledger and story bank
- Versioned editorial knowledge pack

**Acceptance**

- Every source has locator/hash/rights/use limits
- Original transcript remains separate from edits and translations
- Claims retain units, dates, limitations and evidence links
- Each client has a separate allowlist and storage boundary
- A second reviewer checks substantive evidence before promotion

**Measures**

- Draft source coverage
- Correction rate
- Approval cycle time
- Knowledge freshness

**Dependencies**

- Client-approved documents and recording authority
- Exact storage and access scope

**Boundaries**

- A knowledge pack is not model weight training
- No broad device ingestion, cross-client memory or automatic private-data uploads

**Skills:** pdf, doc, documents, knowledge-memory-routing, data-quality-frameworks, human-like-rewrite-with-fact-preservation

**Tools:** Bundled pypdf/python-docx and spreadsheet readers, pdftotext, Whisper, ripgrep, Local Markdown/JSON/SQLite, Notion or Google Drive connector when authorized
