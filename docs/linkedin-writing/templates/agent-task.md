## Work to perform

- Identify the strongest supportable point and label its claim type.
- Check that the selected pattern's evidence burden is met. If a founder story or measured case is unsupported, preserve the gap and propose a suitable lower-burden structure.
- Build the hook-to-payoff contract and assign functions to the actual paragraphs, panels or scenes. Do not enforce a fixed count.
- Keep the evidence, warrant, boundary and conclusion connected. An analogy, plausible mechanism or second agent's agreement is not an independent source.
- Preserve the author's stance, uncertainty and technical distinctions. Never fabricate a memory, quotation, customer or outcome.
- Use optional humor or audience interaction only when it serves this reader and fits the author. A joke can end on its payoff.
- Reconcile every changed factual passage with the claim ledger. Record source access honestly, including abstract-only or derived-assessment-only access.

## Editor and author handoffs

| Role | Responsibility | Returns work when |
|---|---|---|
| Source assessor | Identify evidence, access scope, support and material gaps. | A requested claim exceeds the material actually available. |
| Structure writer | Select the pattern and develop the hook, paragraph functions, reasoning and ending. | The intended story or argument requires missing evidence. |
| Voice editor | Preserve the author's approved stance and rhythm while improving clarity. | A rewrite changes a fact, modality, quotation or implied experience. |
| Independent reviewer | Inspect the relevant sources and review the exact draft against the gates. | Evidence, voice, source or privacy issues remain unresolved. |
| Author / commissioning editor | Confirm personal experience, protected wording, disclosure and the exact final expression. | An attributed first-person statement or release decision lacks confirmation. |

One person or agent may perform more than one production role when the task allows it. Record the actual reviewer; do not call a self-review independent or claim model diversity without evidence.
