# Outspoken 30-second motion film: specification (proposed)

Status: **proposed** for owner review; owner acceptance is false. Written 2 November 2026. Companion files: `VOICEOVER-30S.md`, `voiceover-30s.txt`, `captions-30s.srt`. Existing 80-second film: `../README.md`, `../SPEC.md`.

## 1. What this film is
A 30.00 s motion-graphics film for LinkedIn (muted autoplay first, sound second) and the website. It tells the website's story in one breath: *you know your product; buyers decide in seconds; we start from your experience, find the buyer's question, write it in your voice, show it clearly, and connect profile, content and campaigns into one message.* It ends on the protected hero line and the call to action. It must be **ready for a voiceover**: the picture is locked to the timing table in `VOICEOVER-30S.md`, a muted guide track and the music are included, and a recorded voice can be laid on top without retiming.

Deliverables (rendered locally, not committed as binaries): `cut30-9x16.mp4` (1080x1920, primary), `cut30-16x9.mp4` (1920x1080, website and desktop feed), optionally `cut30-4x5.mp4` (1080x1350). H.264 High, yuv420p, BT.709, 30 fps, AAC 48 kHz, music and effects only (no voice), plus `cut30-guide.mp4` with the synthetic guide voice for timing review, plus a contact sheet and QA report. The repository holds the source, the timing, the audio synthesis code and the QA report.

## 2. Lessons from the example film (fonio application video, 98 s, 1080x1920) and what we do differently
Reviewed with ffmpeg: contact sheet every 3 s, scene-change count (15 changes in 98 s), audio extracted. It is a clean, readable, vertical piece. **Take the principles, never the content:** a progress header with chapter counters, large kinetic captions with the *current word emphasised*, one metaphor object per beat, UI cards built from the product's own visuals, a dark "emphasis" section between light sections, a terminal-style end card.
Where it is weaker, and ours must be better:
- Long stretches of static composition (a card sits still for 5 to 8 s). Ours changes the picture every 1 to 2.5 s and always has a camera or particle drift.
- Hard cuts and soft fades only. Ours uses **radical, designed transitions**: ink flood, spark burst, paint wipe, whip pan with light streaks, selection-handle expansion (section 4).
- Captions and UI chrome compete for the eye. Ours keeps one focal object per beat, captions in the safe zone, and nothing smaller than 40 px at 1080 wide.
- Real-person video carries the example. Ours uses only fictional, labelled people and the brand's own objects: no real person, no third-party logo, no real LinkedIn UI.

## 3. Look (Outspoken design system V10, proposed values)
Navy `#162760` and deep navy `#0D1A3B` for the dark acts; pearl `#F1F3F6` for light acts; nickel `#A9AFBA`; marigold `#EAA93C` (accent and sparks), marigold 300 `#F6D591` (marker fill), marigold 400 `#F3C15D` (text on navy); cobalt `#3157D5` only as a faint glow. Fraunces 400 for display (tracking -2 %), Mona Sans 400/600 for text, Geist Mono 500 uppercase for labels and counters. Dotted ground `radial-gradient(rgba(22,39,96,.13) 1px, transparent 1.5px)` at 22 px (scale with the frame). The selected-text accent from the website (marker fill sweeping in, dashed outline, four square handles) is the film's signature gesture. Matte surfaces, hairline borders, radii 14 to 22 px, soft shadows; no gloss, no glass, no neon, no purple or teal. Wordmark: the supplied B01 lockup (`assets/public/assets/identity/v10/lockup-b01.svg` and `-inverse`), never re-typeset. Icons: D05 sprite (`icons-d05.svg`). Graded fictional portraits: `assets/identity/v10/heroes/*.webp` (labelled "Illustrative").
Motion language: brand ease `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; settle with a very small overshoot (<= 3 %); continuous micro-motion (slow push-in 1.00 to 1.06 per scene, 8 to 14 px parallax drift on background layers). Every transition starts 4 to 8 frames **before** the outgoing beat ends so cuts feel continuous. Flashes: at most 3 frames, at most 3 per second.

## 5. Audio
Synthesise in code (numpy and scipy, same approach as `scripts/audio/` of the 80-second film): a calm, modern pulse with soft piano-like and pad layers, no drums on the finale, loudness build toward 21.3, a clear 1.2 s tail at the end. Transition sound effects: ink (low swell and wet noise), sparks (filtered noise crackle and a high sweep), paint (soft swish), whip (band-passed noise sweep), flood (sub swell). **Stems** (`music.wav`, `sfx.wav`, `guide-voice.wav`) are kept separate so the recorded voice can be added; the delivered mix keeps room for speech (music ducked 8 to 10 dB during speech windows). Target -16 LUFS integrated for the voice-ready mix, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP. State plainly that the score and guide voice are synthetic stand-ins.

## 6. Implementation
Two engines are acceptable; use exactly one for the master and say which. **HyperFrames** (HTML, CSS and GSAP, deterministic seek, rendered through headless Chrome and FFmpeg) is preferred **if** the feasibility test in `skills/README.md` passed on the build machine. Otherwise use the existing **Remotion** project in `film/` (kit in `src/kit`, new scenes under `src/cut30/`, compositions `Cut30Vertical`, `Cut30Wide`, `Cut30Square45`, registered in `src/Root.tsx`, render with `render.mjs`). Whatever the engine:
- Everything is driven by the frame number; no wall-clock time, no `Math.random()` (seed all particles); two renders of the same frame must be byte-identical.
- Gooey ink uses an SVG filter (Gaussian blur plus colour-matrix threshold) over circles; sparks are seeded particles drawn as additive streaks with a trail; the paint wipe is a clip path with a noisy edge and droplets; the whip pan uses a horizontal blur plus streaks; the selection expansion reuses the website's geometry.
- Layout comes from one `layout` object per aspect ratio (9:16, 16:9, 4:5): the same scenes re-composed, not scaled.
- Optional image overlays from Codex pack R11 (E1 sparks and streaks on black with screen blend, E2 ink and paint splashes on white or black with multiply or screen, F4 grain overlays) may be added **only** from `assets/inbox/` after ingestion; the film must also render without them.
- Reduced load: render at concurrency 2 to 3 on an idle machine, then run the frame-difference outlier check (no garbled frames).

## 7. QA gates (all measured and recorded in `renders/cut30-qa.json`)
Duration 30.00 s +/- 1 frame; frame size and codec as listed; no frame with luminance outlier or tile corruption; no flash above 3 per second or 3 frames; text never closer than 5 % to an edge (9:16: bottom 20 % free of text); smallest text at least 40 px at 1080 wide; every fictional person and post labelled; contrast pairs from the design system (pearl on navy 12.65, marigold-400 on navy 8.44, marigold-400 on deep navy 10.26, navy on marigold-300 9.94 (computed)); loudness and peak as above; determinism check on 5 sampled frames; the voice-ready mix has no clipping. Report PASS or FAIL with numbers; never claim a check that was not run.
