Status: **proposed wording** for owner approval. The only protected sentence is the hero line "Make the right buyers care about you and what you’re building." and the call to action "Book an intro call", both used exactly as on the website. Nothing here claims a result, a client or a statistic.

- Film length: **30.00 s** (900 frames at 30 fps). Speech: **62 words in 25.9 s of planned speech** (about 2.4 words per second, a calm commercial pace). The rest is pauses and visual-only beats.
- Language: English. Delivery: warm, close to the microphone, confident and unhurried. The picture and the transitions carry the energy; the voice does not shout. Smile on the last line.
- Pronunciation: "Outspoken" (out-SPOH-ken), LinkedIn (LINK-d-in).

## How to record
1. Read the whole text once as one take (`voiceover-30s.txt`), then re-read any line that needs it. Leave 0.5 s of room tone before and after each take.
2. 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono, no processing, no clipping.
3. Respect the planned gaps between lines: they are where the picture changes. A line may start up to 0.3 s late or early; the picture is built to absorb that.
4. Deliver the takes as `S1.wav` to `S7.wav` (one file per scene) plus the full read as `full.wav`.
5. The film is built so that the voiceover is the last step: it ships with a muted guide track and room for the voice. Mixing target: speech about -16 LUFS integrated, music ducked 8 to 10 dB under speech, true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP.
