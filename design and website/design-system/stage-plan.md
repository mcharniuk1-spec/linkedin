## Stage 2 delivery — local owner review

The local delivery uses the shared UI, assets, tokens, Motion and Three.js runtime across Home, Services, Contact and Confirmation. Desktop, phone and intermediate runtime checks accompany the editable desktop/phone Figma frames. The contact flow validates locally and confirms that no message was sent or booking made. No deployment is included. Continue through the writing route for any authorized copy correction; preserve the protected hero, VP and CTA.

The preserved homepage narrative brief is:

1. White ground, cobalt founder silhouette and quiet team. The phone represents transmitting expertise to an audience. Approved logo/hero/VP/CTA remain the reading priority. A thin-frame video supports the scene; target roughly 20–25% of desktop viewport area, with deliberate mobile rearrangement.
2. A bounded native-scroll chapter withdraws support objects, approaches and rotates above the founder, then transitions into deep blue. Camera motion must correspond to actual spatial geometry; keep an honest static fallback.
3. Many restrained luminous points form a volumetric brain-shaped constellation. The scene holds within a bounded section while 3–7 lines progressively introduce experience, customer insight, product judgment, methods/tools, cases/evidence, convictions and market perspective, selected from real buyer/interview context.
4. Express the idea in natural, reviewed language: expertise that stays in the founder's head cannot help buyers judge its value. Interviews reveal the material; positioning gives it a buyer context; reviewed content makes it understandable. This is a proposed narrative, not a claim of neurological analysis.
5. The light-cloud recedes while the cards persist and assemble into a native publication/artwork example. Explain interview → strategy → content → founder approval → ready-to-post delivery, followed by meaningful offer, process, objections/FAQ and conversion blocks.
6. Permissioned feedback, or clearly fictional examples, appears in varied email/comment cards from both sides. Adapt the Wall of Love idea without copying assets. Support reading dwell, hover/focus pause and a static list. A soft hover highlight is allowed; busy flying blocks are not the purpose.

No scroll traps or forced page locks. Use bounded sticky sections, native scrolling, accessible document-order content, reversible/resizable animation and reduced-motion alternatives. Preserve readable controls and content throughout. The website only becomes ready after actual local runtime, desktop/mobile, keyboard/focus, copy, motion/performance and visual review.
