## What the sample says about format (descriptive, not causal)
In the reference sample (`reports/20261031/`, 279 posts from 61 accounts, counters captured 20 to 22 October 2026) the median reactions were: image posts 186.5 (111 posts), document posts 178 (9 posts), text posts 94 (135 posts), video posts 30 (24 posts). The video group is small and the authors differ, so this is no proof that video performs worse. It does suggest: lead with one clear idea in the first two lines, upload the film natively with captions on, and keep the carousel drafts (`TOPICS-AND-PUBLICATIONS-R12.md`, P1 to P3) as the main series.
