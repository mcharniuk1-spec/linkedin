# LinkedIn publication architecture

Turn a source packet into an original, reviewable publication. Start with the user's requested scope: a structure can be complete while missing evidence still blocks a factual caption. Use the supplied author and buyer brief; a client's buyers do not inherit an agency's audience.

Read [the composition contract](references/composition-contract.md) for pattern choice, proof and review. Read [the interest and format contract](references/interest-and-format.md) when revising hooks, humor, captions or visuals. If working in the Outspoken repository, consult its current writing architecture and source/claim ledgers as project evidence. These packaged references are sufficient when that repository is absent.

Read [the practical drafting and knowledge route](references/practical-drafting.md) for evidence-card retrieval, recurring strategy briefs, useful examples, founder voice and caption-to-visual handoff. Use the current project knowledge when supplied; the skill still works from a standalone source packet.

## Compose

1. Write the reader's concrete question or decision and the strongest supportable point in ordinary language. Separate topic, reader job, publication pattern, media format and voice. Use one dominant job; a post need not be a story.
2. Inventory claims and their exact support before drafting a dramatic opening. Keep observation, attribution, interpretation, recommendation, forecast, recollection and illustration distinct. A missing founder event remains missing. Use a lower-burden method or explicitly hypothetical example when appropriate.
3. Choose a pattern and functional sequence from the composition reference. Explain why the evidence supports the point, and where that inference stops. Keep the conditions that would change the conclusion. A before/after number or screenshot does not establish cause or repeatability.
4. Draft two openings for the same supported material. Name each opening's payoff. Repair a promise/count mismatch, or narrow the hook; never invent support to fulfill it.
5. Give each paragraph, panel or scene a contribution. Use a reverse outline to find repeated claims, missing reasoning and a second competing thesis. Paragraph and panel counts follow the explanation, not a quota.
6. End with an earned conclusion, useful application, real open question or relevant offer. A completed thought needs no engagement request. Preserve any exact required CTA. Do not add guarantees or risk reversals absent from the approved offer.
7. Edit for the author's supported vocabulary, stance, detail and rhythm. Preserve names, quantities, dates, technical terms, exact quotations, links, citations and uncertainty. A better-sounding unsupported fact is a regression.

## Return a reviewable result

Include the publication ID/revision, intended reader/job, pattern, openings and payoff, outline or requested caption/panel copy, claim/evidence/reasoning/limit map, ending and unresolved inputs. For an edit, identify material changes and the protected facts that survived. Use the user's output format when supplied; do not impose a large schema on a simple edit.

Separate `structure_complete_for_review` from `draft_ready_for_author_review`. Missing factual support, personal confirmation, private-detail permission or caption/media reconciliation must remain visible holds. The author accepts their actual stance, experience and final revision. Editorial review does not itself authorize publication.

When reviewing a batch, compare the actual reader questions and new contributions. Distinguish repeated topics with new decisions from duplicate posts. Merge, narrow or defer overlap instead of manufacturing another anecdote, statistic or joke. A cold reader may restate the point, support, limit and next action; record actual review, not a simulated customer study.
