# Composition and proof contract

## Choose by reader job

| Pattern | Useful sequence | Required material |
|---|---|---|
| P01 Evidence-led insight | observation → scope → meaning → practical question or implication | Source, denominator, period and missingness; alternatives to the interpretation. |
| P02 Founder decision story | moment → goal/constraint → plausible alternatives → choice/reason → observed consequence → reflection/transfer | Author-confirmed episode and permission; distinguish knowledge then from interpretation now. |
| P03 Changed mind or mistake | prior belief → action → conflicting signal → consequence → revised explanation → changed practice | Actual prior belief and episode; a poor outcome does not automatically prove a poor decision. |
| P04 Workflow walkthrough | trigger → input → steps/decision → output/reviewer → next action/limit | Actual method or clearly proposed/illustrative process; retain technical conditions that matter. |
| P05 Diagnostic checklist | recognizable problem → discriminating checks → interpretation → next check | Relevant criteria; a diagnostic is not proof of prevalence or performance. |
| P06 Research translation | practical question → study/design → finding → applicability/alternatives → bounded implication | Exact source access and study population; an abstract is not a full-method audit. |
| P07 Qualified counterposition | strong opposing advice → useful conditions → limit → better criterion → example | Fair attribution or clearly described position; no invented quotation or universal reversal. |
| P08 Measured case/customer proof | problem/baseline → intervention → observed result → attribution/limits → application | Real permitted case; numerator/denominator, units, period, comparator and intervention context. |
| P09 Build update/release | change → why → inspectable example → availability/limits → appropriate next step | Exact product version/status; demonstration is distinct from reliable operation. |
| P10 Comparison/review | decision → shared criteria → alternatives → evidence/trade-offs → conditional choice | Comparable conditions, disclosed access and meaningful exclusions. |
| P11 Working question/discussion | bounded uncertainty → known evidence/options → question worth answering | A real unresolved question; replies/polls are self-selected, not representative demand evidence. |
| P12 Offer/invitation | fit → relevant problem → actual scope/input/output → proof/limits → next step | Approved capability and current offer; positioning is not delivered client performance. |
| P13 Focused professional observation | concrete distinction → example → why it matters → earned close | A bounded viewpoint, not a fabricated survey or autobiography. |
| P14 Professional humor/recognition | recognizable situation → expectation → small reversal/payoff | Author-appropriate voice; real episodes confirmed or fiction clearly signaled. A moral is optional. |

These sequences describe functions. Combine or reorder them when the explanation remains clear. Do not fill every cell with prose merely to satisfy a template.

## Claim record

For each material claim retain: claim ID and wording; type; source locator/version/date; what was actually read, viewed or measured; exact support; why it bears on the claim; operating conditions; alternatives; rights/private-detail state; and disposition. Use source-confirmed, attributed, proposed, illustrative, missing or unsupported states as appropriate. Unknown is distinct from measured zero.

A numerical observation needs its unit, sample/denominator, time window and comparison. Calculate any percentage from the stated quantities, preserve its rounding and check the reference population. A single demonstration can support what happened in that demonstration; it cannot establish a typical customer saving or robust percentage promise. Do not turn association, selection or a before/after observation into cause.

For a factual story, confirm the author, actual event, goal, constraint, alternatives considered, decision, reason at that time, observed outcome and permission. Never supply plausible dialogue, motives, emotion, a customer, an earlier belief or a triumphant ending when absent. Another person's thoughts need attribution, not narrative omniscience.

When a source narrows a claim, revise the claim, opening and conclusion together. Review exact quotations against context. Preserve uncertainty and necessary technical distinctions during natural-language editing.

## Review order

Check source/claim fit and inference, then structure/payoff, then voice/interest, then caption/media consistency. A critical factual or rights defect cannot be offset by style. For every actual panel, compare visible text with the caption's current claim and source labels. Removing an unsupported statistic from a caption while retaining it in a panel leaves the combined publication on hold.

In a short revision, return the better wording plus any real hold. Do not burden already-good copy with a new formula or fabricated specificity.
