## When you open a form

1. Open the linked official CTA yourself and read its privacy notice and every field label before entering anything. The public observation here does not establish form fields or data retention.
2. If a form requires an organization, budget, sales objective, or purchase timeline, answer only with true information. If the form cannot represent a research-only enquiry truthfully, stop and use published material instead.
3. Use optional free-text fields to state the purpose plainly. Describe your actual question. Do not add a fictitious buying deadline or client brief.
4. If a call is accepted, repeat the research purpose at the start and ask whether the agency is comfortable answering. End the call if they require a buyer conversation.
5. Save only public service details and the agency's response that you are authorized to retain. Do not record or redistribute confidential or personal information.
