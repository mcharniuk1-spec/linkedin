# OUTSP-9: one call at a time

4. If a call is appropriate, choose a slot yourself and enter only truthful required details. No booking was made by the agent.

## A30-minute conversation

Opening: “I’m evaluating support for my own LinkedIn presence and services. I’d like to understand what the founder supplies, what your team does, what needs approval and how the monthly scope is priced. I prefer to keep organization details private at this point. Is that enough context for this conversation?”

Ask in this order:

1. **Output:** “What exactly would be delivered in the first month and each month afterward?” Capture posts, formats, revision limits, profile, engagement and research separately.
2. **Source:** “What does the founder provide, how long is the interview, and how do you capture voice and product facts?”
3. **Example:** “Can you walk me through a public or redacted example from interview note to approved post and final graphic?”
4. **Review:** “Who writes, edits, designs, fact-checks and approves? What happens when a claim is unclear?”
5. **Access/tools:** “Which steps need LinkedIn access? Which are manual, scheduled or automated? Do you require credentials, a role/integration, Sales Navigator or client-paid tools? Is a delivery-only option available?” Ask how they handle platform restrictions; a vendor's assurance is not verification of permission.
6. **Outreach:** “Who defines targeting and messages, handles replies, decides qualification and hands a meeting brief to sales?” For content-only scope, skip this.
7. **Proof:** “Which report fields are actual account data, and which show qualified conversations or commercial outcomes? Can you share a redacted example and the attribution limits?”
8. **Price/terms:** “What are setup and recurring fees, currency/tax basis, seats/tool costs, minimum term, cancellation notice and exclusions? Can you send the scope and terms in writing?”

Closing: “Thank you. Please send the scope and any public or redacted examples you are comfortable sharing. I’m still comparing approaches and will review the information.”

## Record immediately after each call

Agency/date/source/contact channel; actual statements; materials received and permission; content scope; outreach scope; roles; founder time; review/revision limits; tools and payer; account access; setup/monthly price/currency/taxes/term; claimed result and its evidence; unknowns; follow-up question. Distinguish quotation from paraphrase and inference. Do not record a call without appropriate consent. Keep named replies and unpublished materials in private storage.
